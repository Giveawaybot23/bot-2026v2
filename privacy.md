# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data [Bot Name] ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how you can request that it be deleted. By using the Bot in a Discord server, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Data We Collect

When you interact with the Bot, we may store the following:

- **Discord User ID** — used as the key to identify your account within the Bot's game data. This is not a Discord password, email, or any other Discord account credential.
- **Discord Username** — displayed alongside actions like auction bids and listings so other players can see who they're interacting with.
- **In-game data**, including:
  - Virtual currency balance
  - Item/plant collection and inventory
  - Trade and auction history (items offered, bid amounts, timestamps)
  - Market listings
  - XP, level, and achievement progress
  - Cooldown timers, streaks, and daily/weekly claim history
  - User-configured settings (e.g., autosell rules, equipped items, titles)

## 2. Message Content

The Bot reads the text of messages sent in servers where it is active **only to detect commands** (messages beginning with `!`) and specific gameplay keywords (such as "claim"). Message content is processed in memory to determine what command you're running and is **not logged, stored, or retained** by the Bot beyond what is needed to execute that command in the moment. Message content is never used to train machine learning or AI models.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

Data described in Section 1 is used solely to operate the Bot's gameplay features — maintaining your inventory and currency, running auctions and trades, calculating leaderboards, and enforcing cooldowns and limits. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Where Data Is Stored

Game data is stored locally on the servers that host the Bot, in structured data files. It is not shared with third parties, except as necessary to operate the Bot (e.g., Discord's own API, which the Bot relies on to function).

## 5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you continue to use the Bot in a server where it is active, so that your progress and inventory persist between sessions. Inactive accounts may be periodically cleaned up at the Bot operator's discretion.

## 6. Your Rights & Deletion Requests

You can request that your data be deleted at any time. To do so, contact a server administrator with access to the Bot, who can remove your account data using the Bot's built-in data-wipe tools. Alternatively, you can reach out directly to [contact method — e.g. email or Discord support server invite].

Once deleted, your currency, inventory, trade history, and other game progress cannot be recovered.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own Terms of Service, which require users to meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet these requirements.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
