How it works

A living, breathing
plant economy.

Every plant has a version number. Lower versions are rarer, worth more, and never come back once claimed.

🌱
Claim Drops
Plants drop in real-time in Discord. Type the captcha first to claim it — speed matters.
🔄
Trade
Live trading sessions let you swap plants and coins with other collectors. Build your dream collection.
📦
Open Crates
Five crate tiers from Bronze to Ruby. Each opens 10 plants with dramatically different odds.
📈
Market Demand
Frequently traded plants increase in value. Watch the market and sell at the right time.
🏆
Garden Score
Your weighted score accounts for rarity, mutations, and version. Climb from Iron to Celestial tier.
⚠️
Decay System
Go inactive for 3 days and plants start disappearing. v1–v10 are always safe, but the rest aren't.
